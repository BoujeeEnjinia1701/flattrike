# BOM notes

- All costs are indicative USD prices for a single prototype, estimated at TRL 2. They will be replaced with quotes from suppliers in the target city at TRL 3.
- Item numbers 1 to 12 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Items 13 to 15 are not modelled.
- Plate costs assume 3 mm S235JR or A36 class steel at about $1.2 per kg and small-batch laser cutting. At volume, cutting cost per frame falls sharply.
- Base prototype (items 1 to 14): about $640, within the $800 budget in `project.yaml`.
- Item 15, the electric assist kit, is optional and **not** included in the base cost. With it the prototype would cost about $1,040, over budget. The assist option and its battery (generic pack or SwapCell) are proposed, awaiting Amish.
